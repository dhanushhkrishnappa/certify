Let’s approach this from a different angle.

Consider two isolated nodes, Node A and Node B. Node A executes event E1 at scalar tick 3. Simultaneously, Node B executes event E2 at scalar tick 4 without a single packet passing between them.

Why does a scalar clock mislead us into thinking E1 caused E2? Because scalar numbers always provide a total order (3 < 4), conflating arbitrary serialization with physical causality.
